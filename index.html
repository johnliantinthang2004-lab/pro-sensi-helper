Test and tune

  1. Open Training Mode and spray at a wall about 30 m away. Recoil should feel controllable, not twitchy.
  2. Track a moving target at about 100 m with a 2x or 3x scope. If you overshoot, lower those scope values.
  3. Do quick 180 degree turns. If you can't turn fast enough, raise Camera. If you overshoot, lower it.
  4. Change one thing at a time, in steps of 3 to 5, and play a few matches before changing again.

These are starting points, not official or pro settings. Every screen and hand is different. Your choices are saved on this device.
